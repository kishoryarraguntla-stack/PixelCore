Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Understand the enquiry process before you proceed.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?

No. An enquiry is only a request for information. The lender independently assesses eligibility, documents, creditworthiness and repayment capacity before deciding.

What interest rate or loan amount can I get?

Rates, APR, loan amount, tenure and charges depend on the lender, product and applicant profile. Review a written offer and the Key Facts Statement before accepting.

Which documents are usually required?

Common documents may include PAN, identity/address proof, income records and bank statements. Business, property or vehicle loans may require additional supporting documents. The lender will confirm the exact checklist.

How long does approval take?

Processing time varies by lender, product, document completeness, verification and property/vehicle checks. No approval or timeline is guaranteed.

Will Digi Loans itself provide the loan?

Loan funding and final decisions are made only by the relevant lending institution. Before proceeding, confirm the lender’s legal name, regulatory status, terms and official contact details.

Are there any fees?

Any applicable lender fees, charges, interest and APR should be clearly disclosed in the lender’s official documents. Do not pay an unknown person or personal account, and do not rely on verbal promises.

How is my personal information handled?

Only provide information through verified, secure channels and after reading the applicable privacy notice. Do not submit OTPs, passwords, card PINs or banking login credentials.

Can I cancel after receiving an offer?

Read the lender’s agreement and applicable cooling-off/look-up and cancellation terms. Ask the lender for the procedure and any applicable charges before accepting.
