Our services

Complete care for every smile.

From preventive check-ups to restorative and cosmetic dentistry, every treatment starts with a clear conversation about your needs.

Pain-Free Tooth Extraction

Pain-free tooth extraction with careful clinical assessment and patient comfort in mind.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment to help preserve affected natural teeth.

Correction of Irregular & Crooked Teeth

Care for irregular and crooked teeth with an appropriate treatment plan.

Fixed Teeth

Fixed tooth replacement options planned according to individual dental needs.

Bad Breath Care

Assessment and care for oral odour and related dental or gum concerns.

Gum Disease Care

Evaluation and treatment support for gum disease and gum health.

Bleeding Gum Care

Composite / GIC Fillings

Tooth-coloured composite and GIC filling options for suitable cases.

Professional Teeth Cleaning

Professional cleaning to help remove deposits and maintain a cleaner smile.
